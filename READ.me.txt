Olist E-Commerce Data Analysis

Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing the Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist to gain insights into the performance of an online marketplace.

The analysis covers different business dimensions, including sales, customers, products, orders, payments, and delivery operations.

The main objective is to explore business performance, identify trends and patterns, and present meaningful insights through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

Business Context

Olist is a Brazilian e-commerce marketplace that connects small businesses with online sales channels and supports order fulfillment through logistics partners.

The dataset contains approximately 100,000 orders made between 2016 and 2018, providing information across multiple aspects of the e-commerce business.

Project Objectives

Analyze overall sales and order performance.

Explore customer purchasing behavior.

Identify product and category trends.

Analyze payment methods and patterns.

Evaluate delivery performance.

Present meaningful insights to support business decision-making.

Tools & Technologies

SQL / MySQL: Data querying and exploration.

Microsoft Excel: Data review and preparation.

Power BI: Data visualization and interactive dashboard development.

Data Preparation

Data cleaning and transformation were performed to improve data quality and prepare the dataset for analysis.
